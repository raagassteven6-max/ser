The pixel GIFs I picked for Zen Corner's scenes and backdrops. I'm a big fan of both of these artists.

::: credit
title: Waneella
url: https://waneella.tumblr.com/
link: Tumblr
image: https://api.tumblr.com/v2/blog/waneella.tumblr.com/avatar/128

**I really love her art style and vibes. The atmosphere is just pure Anemoia.**

Anemoia is that odd ache for a time you never lived in, and her scenes give me exactly that. I look at them and feel nostalgic for places that don't exist.

Her blog is all pixel art, and a lot of it moves. Tiny loops, light shifting, a scene breathing. It also mentions Shibuya Pixel Art and some game art, so she's clearly doing this for real and not just for fun. I'm glad I found her.
:::

::: credit
title: Motocross-Arts
url: https://motocross-arts.tumblr.com/
link: Tumblr
image: https://api.tumblr.com/v2/blog/motocross-arts.tumblr.com/avatar/128

**This gives me catharsis. Like a light feeling after I dump all the weights. It's just soo goooood 😇**

Where Waneella's work makes me wistful, this one makes me feel lighter. The blog describes the artist as a pixel artist and designer in Japan, with animated GIFs as the main thing.

I can't read all of the Japanese on the blog, but you don't need to read pixels. They hit you either way.
