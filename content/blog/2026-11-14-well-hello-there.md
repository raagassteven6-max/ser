# Well, Hello There! 👋

*My first blog post. Let's see where this goes.*
:::

---

Well, this is my first blog post!

I'm not much of a writer, let alone a blogger. Although I do write fictional high-fantasy stories and books from time to time, they're mostly just amateur projects. Nothing too serious.

But hey, this is a start. Consider this a little test run. 😆

This website is actually my **fourth design so far**. Yes, fourth. I've gone through quite a few iterations trying to figure out what I wanted this little corner of the internet to look like.

I'm used to writing HTML and CSS from scratch. I've always preferred building things myself because I want everything to look and behave exactly the way I envision it. I've browsed templates here and there, of course, but there's something different about putting everything together yourself.

Although, with the emergence of AI, things have become considerably easier. It's been a huge help in speeding up development and taking care of some of the more tedious work. For this particular website, I used Claude to help put things together. That saved me quite a bit of time since I could focus more on reviewing, tweaking, and testing rather than writing every single line myself.

**A little less time coding, a little more time building.** ✨

## A Little About Me

I've been a self-taught developer since I was around 16. I'm 29 now, as of the day I'm writing this.

I never had a standard educational background in software development or engineering, and I don't have a formal degree in either. So, I don't particularly like putting myself into a specific category. Frontend? Backend? Full-stack? 

I suppose *full-stack* would be the closest thing. 🤣

But honestly, I dabble in just about anything that catches my interest.

From websites and web applications to AI integrations, automations (both pre-built and custom), and even fine-tuning AI models for specific use cases and agentic workflows.

I've also spent time working on data curation and feature engineering for AI model training and fine-tuning.

:::tip
**And that last one?**

Probably the hardest thing I've ever decided to get myself into. Seriously. 😖
:::

It's one thing to build an application around an AI model, but getting involved in the data side of things is an entirely different beast.

Of course, all of these are hobbies and personal pursuits. I'm not a professional developer, nor have I worked for a company or anyone else as a developer. Most of what I know comes from learning, experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and occasionally wondering why something worked in the first place.

I honestly still don't know how to begin turning all of this into something more.

But that's a story for another day.

## About This Little Website

As for this website, it's a fairly generic 90s desktop-inspired design. I wanted something that felt a little nostalgic without necessarily recreating an actual operating system.

I'm not much of a web designer, after all. I'm more comfortable working behind the scenes, dealing with logic, systems, integrations, and all that fun stuff.

The entire website is also built around a **single HTML file**, with its content loaded separately. It's a simple approach, but it works for what I have in mind.

And honestly, that's what matters.
