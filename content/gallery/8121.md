# Spider Lily

*A flower I've loved for years, and one I've chosen to keep close.*
:::

---

## 01 — A Flower I've Always Loved

A special addition to the collection: **Spider Lily**.

I've always loved this flower. I remember first coming across it years ago in a book, and since then I've kept noticing it in all sorts of places. It has such a distinct shape that it's hard to mistake for anything else: those long, delicate stamens reaching outward, the curled petals, and that wonderfully dramatic red-orange colour. Even as a botanical subject, it almost looks like something someone made up.

## 02 — A Little Dramatic, Perhaps

I also see spider lilies pop up in anime quite a lot. They're often given a rather ominous role or used to set a dark mood. Which is funny, because I just think they're gorgeous. 😂

I understand why artists are drawn to that symbolism, but I can't help loving the flower for what it is, too. There's something wonderful about its unusual shape and the way its petals seem to curl and reach in every direction.

## 03 — The Artwork

This print is by **Kawarazaki Shōdō (1889–1973)**, a Japanese artist known for his botanical woodblock prints. His work belongs to the *shin-hanga* tradition, and he is especially associated with flower designs published by Unsōdō.

I love how carefully the plant is observed here. The flowers are full and expressive, while the unopened blooms at the bottom give the composition another little detail to discover. The fine lines of the stamens make the whole arrangement feel almost weightless against the quiet background.

## 04 — The Flower I Chose

But this one is special to me for a reason beyond the artwork.

When I decided to get my second tattoo, I knew I wanted a flower, and I had already settled on a watercolor style. I didn't have to think very hard about which flower to choose. The spider lily was the very first one that came to mind.

It's now on my **right forearm**, and I still love that I chose it. Out of all the flowers I could have picked, this was the one I immediately pictured carrying with me.

## 05 — A Place in the Collection

So having this print in the collection feels a little personal. It's a flower I've admired for years, one I've encountered in books and on screens, and one that eventually made its way onto my skin.

I suppose that's a pretty good reason to give it a place on this wall.
