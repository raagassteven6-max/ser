A delicate study of **ume**, or Japanese plum blossoms, by Japanese artist **Megata Morikaga**, created around 1870–1880.

Slender, dark branches reach across a pale ground, carrying clusters of soft white blossoms and deep red buds. Fine orange details mark the flowers' stamens, while touches of red at the petals and stems give the composition a gentle warmth. The branches are arranged with plenty of open space around them, allowing each bloom to stand out.

The illustration has a quiet, restrained beauty: an attentive botanical study that captures the character of the plum in bloom.
